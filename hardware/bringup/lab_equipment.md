# Lab Equipment

## Minimum Equipment

- Current-limited bench power supply.
- Oscilloscope with sufficient bandwidth for clock and USB-level observation.
- DMM for rail verification.
- Logic analyzer for I2C/SPI control bus inspection.
- USB protocol analyzer (recommended) for bridge troubleshooting.

## RF-Specific Equipment

- RF signal generator or SDR dev board with legal test waveform workflow.
- Spectrum analyzer or VNA for matching and front-end checks.
- Attenuators and coax accessories (75 ohm rated) for controlled injection.

## Software Tools

- Host backend and CLI from this repository.
- Structured log capture.
- Automated replay and validation scripts.
